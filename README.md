# 💰 Revenue Generation Analysis Dashboard (Tableau)

## 📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive **Revenue Generation Analysis Dashboard** using **Tableau** to analyze revenue performance, customer revenue, monthly trends, payment methods, regions, product plans, and revenue streams.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of revenue generation and business performance through interactive visualizations and KPI metrics, helping identify key revenue trends, high-performing regions, payment methods, product plans, and revenue streams for data-driven decision-making.

## 📈 Business Insights

- Total revenue generated was **1,002,000**.
- Average revenue per customer was approximately **3,222**.
- **October** recorded the highest monthly revenue among the analyzed months.
- **Subscription** was the highest revenue-generating stream.
- **Australia** generated the highest revenue among the analyzed regions.
- **Net Banking** generated the highest revenue among the payment modes.
- **One-time service – consulting** was one of the highest revenue-generating product plans.
- Monthly revenue growth showed significant fluctuations throughout the year, highlighting periods of both strong growth and decline.
